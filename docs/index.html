Privacy Policy for FastKeys

Last Updated: January 24, 2026

Introduction

FastKeys ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that provides text expansion and autofill functionality. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

FastKeys collects and stores the following data:

  • Templates: Text templates you create with triggers (e.g., _vpn)
  • Tags: Tags you create to organize templates
  • Profile Variables: Personal information you choose to store (first name, last name, email, custom variables)
  • Settings: Extension settings including language preferences and custom date formats

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally in your Chrome browser using Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.sync or chrome.storage.local)
  • Data may sync across your devices if you're signed into Chrome with sync enabled
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No data is sent to us or any third parties

Data Usage

  • Your templates and data are only used to provide the text expansion functionality within your browser
  • We do not access, read, analyze, or transmit your template content to any external service
  • We do not collect information about your browsing habits
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services

Permissions

FastKeys requests the following permissions:

  • storage: Required to save your templates, tags, and settings locally
  • activeTab: Required to insert expanded text into the current webpage
  • <all_urls>: Required for text expansion to work on any website you visit

These permissions are necessary for the extension's core functionality. We do not use them to collect or transmit data.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Export: You can export all your templates and data using the "Export All" feature
  • Delete: You can delete individual templates or tags at any time
  • Clear All: Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data
  • No Account Required: You don't need to create an account or provide personal information

Third-Party Services

FastKeys does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools. All functionality is self-contained within the extension.

Data Security

  • All data is stored using Chrome's built-in storage API, which is encrypted and secure
  • Data is only accessible to the extension itself
  • We do not have access to your data

Children's Privacy

FastKeys is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when changes were made. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
